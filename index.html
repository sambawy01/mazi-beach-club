A Mediterranean beach club on Egypt's North Coast — wood-fire seafood, a raw bar from the morning boats, and the bright, generous soul of the Greek coast.

Mazi · North Coast

Mazi means together.

Long lunches that melt into golden evenings. Feet in the sand, plates made for sharing, and a table that never really ends. This is Greek and Mediterranean cooking at its most generous — raw-bar catches untouched by fire, whole fish over olive-wood embers, and salads that taste of sun.

Set on the shore of Egypt's North Coast, Mazi is where the day slows down and the sea does the talking.
